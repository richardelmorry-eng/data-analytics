Exercise 3.C - Survey Notes

My survey: Dunkin' Customer Experience Survey (8 questions: 4 multiple choice, 3 star ratings, 1 long text)

Survey I took: Red Robins Customer Feedback Review

Questions I liked:
- "Quick! Which is your go to Red Robin guilty pleasure?" It was fun and matched the brand's personality.
- The 0-10 recommendation scale (Q4) was clear and easy to answer.
- The rewards question (Q6) offered a free appetizer for signing up, which gives customers a reason to join.

Anything confusing:
- Q8 used a heart rating and Q9 used a trophy rating, with no labels. It wasn't clear which end was good.
- Q5 ("There can only be one") didn't say what it was choosing between until the answers appeared.

What I wished I had come up with:
- The branded, playful wording and the different rating icons (stars, hearts, trophies).
- A 0-10 recommendation question.

How I would change or add to it:
- Add labels like "Poor" and "Excellent" to the rating questions.
- Add a follow-up asking why they gave that score.
- Ask for an optional email so customers can be contacted about rewards.
